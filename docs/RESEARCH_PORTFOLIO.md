# Related public Frays research

This document is a guide to **already public** materials and adjacent explorations. It does not imply a unified validated model or a release of private datasets.

| Public study | Question | Where to read | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Learning Lhotse** | Can dialogue preserve unresolved questions, framing and intended outcomes across turns? | [Research protocol](./RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md) and [blog](https://frays.ai/blog/when-a-conversation-stops-answering-its-own-questions) | E0 exploratory agenda and synthetic controls |
| **SOAPS organizational fabric** | Can explicit human–agent relationship contracts improve coordination? | [puchat3k/soaps-organizational-fabric](https://github.com/puchat3k/soaps-organizational-fabric) | Public research protocol, synthetic examples |
| **Lineage: A Collaboration** | How can human–AI collaborations preserve evidence, intent and provenance? | [puchat3k/lineage-a-collaboration](https://github.com/puchat3k/lineage-a-collaboration) | Public concept and local proof design |
| **Atlas Fellowship simulation** | How do conflicting incentives affect synthetic coalition configurations? | [Published Frays field note](https://frays.ai/blog/atlas-fellowship-experiment-coalition-intelligence) | Fictional simulation, not forecasts |
| **Coalition Lab** | Can voluntary, consentful teams collect useful evidence about reciprocal fit? | [Introduction](https://frays.ai/blog/introducing-coalition-lab) | Exploratory participation initiative |

**Research centre:** this repository is the public entry point for Lhotse; additional Frays research can be linked here after source, privacy and licensing review. Other Frays repositories may be private. We do not mirror them or their Git histories.

**Invite feedback:** [read the researcher invitation](https://frays.ai/blog/research-collaborators-learning-lhotse), [open a public issue](https://github.com/puchat3k/learning-lhotse/issues) (only non-sensitive content) or email hello@frays.ai.

**Research/data use:** only synthetic conversations are currently shared in this repository. No individual-level dialogue metrics, inferred characteristics or private transcripts are included.
