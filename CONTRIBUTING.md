# Contributing to Learning Lhotse

We welcome **rigorous criticism**, alternative hypotheses, annotation improvements, reproducible baselines and *newly authored fictional* dialogue controls.

## Good first contributions

- Find a counterexample where apparent semantic drift is justified reframing, refusal or clarification.
- Propose a concise observable annotation rubric that independent evaluators can use without knowing the preferred outcome.
- Compare last reply pair, four-message window and full-history judgments with blinded labels.
- Check and correct academic references with primary scholarly sources.
- Add a truly fictional control: state precisely which observations it is intended to distinguish.

## Before opening an issue or pull request

1. Do **not** paste private conversations, screen captures, correspondence, participant data, signed contracts, unpublished individual scores or employer/customer information into public GitHub.
2. Avoid copying third-party code, datasets or text without an explicit compatible license and provenance.
3. If your contribution depends on nonpublic material, describe the research question abstractly and email **hello@frays.ai** before sharing files.
4. Identify the intended evaluation claim, the simplest competing baseline, potential confounders, and what would falsify the hypothesis. Keep the scope small.

## Intellectual property and collaboration

Contributors retain their background IP and authorship. By intentionally submitting an original contribution for inclusion in this public repository, contributors should have the necessary rights and agree to license that submitted material under the file-type license in [LICENSE.md](./LICENSE.md). A contribution is not automatically accepted, and this open-source contribution route is **not** a transfer of ownership to Frays.

Commissioned or otherwise company-owned work, joint research, publication rights, funding, patents, licensing restrictions or access to protected resources require a **separate written agreement before substantive work begins**. Public comments and expressions of interest do not assign IP, create employment or imply payment.

No researcher has to accept Frays' preferred framing. We specifically value rigorous challenges and well-supported no-build conclusions.

## Scientific integrity

- Clearly label results as synthetic, pilot, independently replicated, or prospectively validated.
- Report unknown or abstained judgments separately from negative scores.
- Preserve original sources privately if authorized; do not retroactively edit precommitted forecasts or labels.
- Do not infer personality, honesty, model authorship or fitness from a Lhotse observation.
